# FOI request: annual referral volumes to NHS gender identity services in England

**Status:** drafted, NOT submitted. Founder action.
**Cost:** nil. **Statutory deadline:** 20 working days from receipt.

## Why this exists

Workstream W02 (NHS referrals, diagnoses and service pathways) is required by 40 of the 64
propositions and is empty, because individual-level NHS data is access-gated behind
processes measured in months. FOI is a parallel, legally-backed route to the *aggregate*
figures, which is what the ascertainment propositions actually need — they are claims about
rates, not about individuals.

The premise is verified, not assumed. An answered request to NHS England, marked
**Successful** on 2026-07-06, is titled "2025 child referral figures by Integrated Care
Boards to Arden and Gem hub & specialist...". NHS England therefore holds this class of
figure and has released it. Others found: phalloplasty referrals by month/year (Arden &
GEM CSU), an East of England Gender Service gamete storage audit (Nottinghamshire
Healthcare, Successful), and a services request to NE & N Cumbria ICB (Successful).

## Who to ask

**Primary — NHS England.** Adult and children's gender services in England are directly
commissioned by NHS England Specialised Commissioning, and referrals route through the
National Referral Support Service run by NHS Arden & Greater East Midlands CSU. Arden & GEM
is hosted by NHS England, so NHS England holds the data and is the correct FOIA addressee.
Asking Arden & GEM directly risks a "not a public authority in its own right" bounce.

**Secondary — Tavistock and Portman NHS Foundation Trust**, for the historic GIDS series up
to the service's closure in March 2024. NHS England may not hold the pre-transfer detail.

**Tertiary, only if NHS England pleads s.12** — one request each to the adult Gender
Dysphoria Clinic providers, each then well inside the cost limit: Nottinghamshire Healthcare
NHS FT, Devon Partnership NHS Trust, Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust, Northamptonshire
Healthcare NHS FT, Sheffield Health & Social Care NHS FT, Cumbria Northumberland Tyne and
Wear NHS FT, Tavistock and Portman NHS FT. For children and young people: Great Ormond
Street Hospital for Children NHS FT and Alder Hey Children's NHS FT.

## The request — send as written

> **Request: Annual referral volumes to NHS gender identity services in England**
>
> I am requesting information under the Freedom of Information Act 2000.
>
> For each financial year from 2018/19 to 2025/26 inclusive, please provide the **total
> number of referrals received** by:
>
> 1. Adult Gender Dysphoria Clinics in England, in total across all providers; and
> 2. NHS Children and Young People's Gender Services in England (including, for years
>    before their transfer, the Gender Identity Development Service), in total across all
>    providers.
>
> Where you hold it in an already-aggregated form, please also break each of the above down
> **by provider organisation** and, separately, **by financial year of referral**.
>
> **To assist you and to keep this request within the appropriate limit:**
>
> - I am asking only for **aggregate counts already held or routinely produced** — for
>   example from the National Referral Support Service operated on your behalf by NHS Arden
>   & Greater East Midlands Commissioning Support Unit, from waiting-list management
>   reporting, or from any existing management information or performance return. **I am not
>   asking you to create new information, to interrogate individual patient records, or to
>   undertake any case-by-case review.** If any part of this request would require that,
>   please answer the remaining parts and tell me which part you have set aside.
> - **I do not seek any personal data.** I am not asking for any information about
>   identifiable individuals, and I am not asking for any breakdown by age, sex, gender,
>   ethnicity, diagnosis, geography below provider level, or any combination of
>   characteristics. Please provide **whole-number counts only, rounded to the nearest 5**.
>   Where a figure is small enough that you consider disclosure could identify an
>   individual, I am content for it to be suppressed and marked as such, and I ask that you
>   still disclose the remaining figures.
> - Please provide the response as a **CSV or Excel spreadsheet** where possible, with one
>   row per provider per financial year.
> - If you consider this request would exceed the appropriate limit under **section 12**,
>   please treat it as a request for **NHS England-wide totals by financial year only**,
>   without any provider breakdown, and please offer advice and assistance under **section
>   16** as to how it could be refined further.
> - If any of this information is already published, please provide a direct link (I
>   appreciate that section 21 may apply), and confirm which years and which figures the
>   link covers.

## Why the wording is shaped this way

**s.12, the cost limit** (£450 / 18 hours for NHS bodies) is the most likely refusal and is
defused three ways: the request asks only for information already held in aggregate, it
explicitly disclaims record-level work, and it carries a pre-authorised fallback scope so
the authority can narrow rather than refuse outright. A large share of s.12 refusals happen
because the requester offered no fallback.

**s.40(2), personal data** is defused by disclaiming personal data, refusing
cross-tabulations — it is the *combination* of characteristics that makes small cells
identifying — asking for rounding to the nearest 5, and pre-consenting to suppression.
Pre-consenting matters: it removes the authority's easiest route to refusing the whole
dataset over a handful of small cells.

**s.21, reasonably accessible** is invited rather than resisted. If they point at a
publication, that is a source we did not know about, which serves W02 either way.

**s.16, advice and assistance** is invoked explicitly, creating a duty to help refine rather
than simply refuse.

## Note on the data when it arrives

Financial years, not calendar years. "2018/19" must not be silently coerced to 2018. A
suppressed cell is missing, not zero — the standing rule in SOURCE_ACCESS_NOTES applies with
particular force here, because a suppressed small cell read as a zero would manufacture
exactly the trough the ascertainment propositions are testing for.
