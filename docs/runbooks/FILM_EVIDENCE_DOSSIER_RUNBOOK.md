# Film evidence dossier runbook

The film is downstream of released research claims.

For each proposed scene:

- bind the narrated statement to a released claim ID;
- list supporting and counterevidence IDs;
- state certainty and the permitted wording;
- state what wording is prohibited;
- identify any participant consent, privacy, copyright, safeguarding or defamation review;
- record the graphic/data source and transformation;
- retain the unresolved uncertainty in the scene notes.

A narrative preference cannot create or upgrade a scientific claim.
