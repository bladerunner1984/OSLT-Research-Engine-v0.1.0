# Claim release standard

A public claim requires:

1. proposition and scope identifier;
2. exact evidence IDs and dependency families;
3. counterevidence and null-lane search status;
4. epistemic status;
5. certainty vector and limiting dimension;
6. attainable inference envelope;
7. approved wording and prohibited overstatement;
8. reproducibility manifest;
9. human review where triggered.

No raw publication count, citation count, model agreement or source prestige may be represented as a
truth score.
