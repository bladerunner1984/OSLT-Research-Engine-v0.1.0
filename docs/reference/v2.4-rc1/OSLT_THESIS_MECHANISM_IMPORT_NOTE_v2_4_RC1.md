# OSLT Thesis Mechanism Import Note v2.4 RC1

## Purpose
The uploaded thesis `THESIS_v55_FORMATTED.pdf` is used as a source of candidate mechanisms, methodological controls and research questions. It is **not** treated as proof of those mechanisms in the OSLT empirical programme.

## Imported methodological architecture
- Critical realism: distinguish observed empirical records, events and hypothesised generative mechanisms.
- Structure–agency separation and different causal timescales.
- Value-transparency: declare investigator priors and manage confirmation-bias risk rather than claiming value-free inquiry.
- Primary Data / Analytical Finding / Theoretical Proposition separation.
- Process tracing, temporal comparison, institutional comparison and jurisdictional comparison.
- Strongest-form counterarguments and explicit falsification conditions.
- Triangulation across evidence families.

## Imported candidate mechanisms
- Influence architecture: salience, defaults, social proof and framing.
- Policy embedding and downstream implementation.
- Network effects and institutional diffusion.
- Spiral of silence and preference falsification.
- Reputational/career consequences as possible behavioural constraints.
- Structural coupling without requiring central coordination.
- Knowledge-production concentration/selection as a testable meta-research hypothesis.
- Macro-cultural/religious displacement as a contextual hypothesis, not an individual causal attribution.

## Translation rule
Every imported mechanism must be converted into:
1. a neutral proposition;
2. a measurable prediction;
3. the strongest rival explanation;
4. a falsifier;
5. required data and timing;
6. an admissible method;
7. a maximum claim ceiling.

The 64-proposition v2.4 registry contains explicit thesis-derived tests `TH01–TH08` plus related competing-model propositions.
