# OSLT Data Acquisition Plan v2.3 RC1

OSLT requires a deliberately heterogeneous evidence estate. The objective is not to collect everything before analysis, but to accumulate enough independent data families to distinguish prevalence change, ascertainment/referral change, developmental heterogeneity, institutional/policy change, social/digital exposure and knowledge/discourse diffusion.

## Acquisition workstreams

### W01 - Population denominators and trend decomposition
**Priority:** P0
**Data:** Population size, age, sex, geography, identity/prevalence measures, survey denominators, time trends
**Candidate source IDs:** DS013;DS014;DS015
**Purpose:** Establish what changed before explaining why; standardise rates and separate prevalence from ascertainment.

### W02 - NHS referrals, diagnoses and service pathways
**Priority:** P0
**Data:** Referral dates, service contacts, diagnoses, procedures, waiting times, provider/geography, outcomes
**Candidate source IDs:** DS001;DS002;DS003;DS004;DS047;DS061;DS062
**Purpose:** Quantify service presentation, ascertainment, pathway change and downstream outcomes.

### W03 - Primary care and psychiatric/neurodevelopmental history
**Priority:** P0
**Data:** GP records, psychiatric diagnoses, autism/ADHD, medication, symptoms, trauma proxies, prior service use
**Candidate source IDs:** DS004;DS005;DS003
**Purpose:** Establish temporal ordering and heterogeneity before/after gender-related presentation.

### W04 - Longitudinal development, family and cohort data
**Priority:** P0/P1
**Data:** Puberty/development, mental health, family, peers, school, social context, repeated measures, genetics where justified
**Candidate source IDs:** DS018;DS020;DS021;DS019;DS007;DS008;DS009
**Purpose:** Reconstruct developmental trajectories and test competing pathway models.

### W05 - Education, safeguarding and school implementation
**Priority:** P0
**Data:** NPD outcomes, school policy, RSHE materials, safeguarding guidance, implementation dates, local variation
**Candidate source IDs:** DS016;DS054;DS055;DS056;DS057
**Purpose:** Test educational-policy exposure and implementation effects using quasi-experimental and multilevel designs.

### W06 - Digital, social-media and peer exposure
**Priority:** P0/P1
**Data:** Platform use, search behaviour, recommendation exposure where obtainable, peer-network measures, media-use surveys
**Candidate source IDs:** DS022;DS023;DS024;DS025;DS026;DS027
**Purpose:** Separate active searching, passive exposure, homophily, peer diffusion and temporal ordering.

### W07 - Academic knowledge-production and publication selection
**Priority:** P0/P1
**Data:** Works, authors, institutions, citations, funding, preregistration, trials, reviews, retractions, publication timing
**Candidate source IDs:** DS033;DS034;DS035;DS036;DS037;DS038;DS039;DS040;DS041;DS042;DS043;DS044;DS059
**Purpose:** Measure publication/citation/guideline diffusion while collapsing shared datasets and research lineages.

### W08 - Historical psychiatry, diagnostic nosology and treatment doctrine
**Priority:** P0/P1
**Data:** DSM/ICD definitions, textbooks/archives, diagnostic criteria, differential diagnosis, treatment recommendations, semantic change
**Candidate source IDs:** DS049;DS050;DS051;DS052;DS060
**Purpose:** Identify regime changes, semantic shifts, mechanism links and historical critical junctures.

### W09 - Clinical guidelines, professional bodies and NHS policy
**Priority:** P0
**Data:** NICE, NHS specifications, Cass implementation, professional guidance, commissioning/service documents
**Candidate source IDs:** DS045;DS046;DS048;DS058;DS063
**Purpose:** Trace how evidence moves into guidance, training, service configuration and clinical practice.

### W10 - Government, legal and parliamentary policy corpus
**Priority:** P0/P1
**Data:** GOV.UK consultations, archived government pages, Hansard, legislation, FOI material
**Candidate source IDs:** DS029;DS030;DS031;DS032;DS057
**Purpose:** Reconstruct policy chronology, institutional framing and implementation mechanisms.

### W11 - Media and public discourse
**Priority:** P1
**Data:** News corpora, web archives, GDELT, lexical/semantic/framing data over time
**Candidate source IDs:** DS028;DS052;DS053;DS060
**Purpose:** Measure discourse, framing and semantic diffusion without treating discourse change as causal proof.

### W12 - Primary qualitative and narrative evidence
**Priority:** P0
**Data:** Longitudinal interviews, clinical/patient narratives where lawfully available, clinician accounts, coded consultation material
**Candidate source IDs:** DS064;DS062
**Purpose:** Test narrative development, mechanism hypotheses and context not captured by administrative data.

### W13 - Designed experiments and replication studies
**Priority:** P0
**Data:** Preregistered vignettes, framing A/B/n, factorial designs, measurement validation, independent replication
**Candidate source IDs:** DS065
**Purpose:** Discriminate explanations where observational evidence cannot identify the mechanism.

## Field-level rule

The source register is a candidate-discovery map, not proof that a required variable exists. Before a source is admitted to a study, OSLT must verify the exact field/instrument, definition, time coverage, missingness, construct validity, linkage key, legal basis, access approval and whether its measurement is comparable across eras/populations.

## Minimum order of operations

1. Freeze outcome definitions, populations, estimands and causal specifications.
2. Acquire open population, policy, historical and literature corpora first.
3. Build the evidence-dependency and historical-concept graphs.
4. Obtain restricted NHS/ONS/DfE/cohort microdata under approved governance.
5. Perform trend/ascertainment decomposition before mechanism attribution.
6. Use independent jurisdictions/cohorts for replication and transportability.
7. Acquire digital/platform data only with clear timing and active/passive exposure distinctions.
8. Commission new primary/experimental studies only for remaining discriminatory evidence gaps.
9. Synthesis occurs only after counterevidence retrieval, contradiction resolution and certainty calibration.
