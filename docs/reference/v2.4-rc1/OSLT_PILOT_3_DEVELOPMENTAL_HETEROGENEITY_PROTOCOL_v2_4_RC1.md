# OSLT Pilot 3 — Developmental Heterogeneity

## Primary question
Are there reproducible developmental, psychiatric, neurodevelopmental and social trajectories that lead to similar gender-related presentations but differ in onset, persistence, treatment pathway or later outcomes?

## Design
Use longitudinal EHR/cohort data plus a deeply characterised primary cohort where feasible. Preserve temporal ordering of autism/ADHD, psychiatric symptoms, puberty/body distress, trauma/safeguarding, peer/digital exposure, first gender-related distress, identity labelling, social transition, referral and treatment.

## Analysis
Measurement invariance/DIF -> trajectory/sequence models -> externally validated latent/heterogeneity models -> prespecified causal analyses within defined pathways -> outcome comparison. Clusters are discovery objects, not clinical subtypes until replicated and externally validated.

## Required comparator trajectories
Continued transition; referral without medical intervention; gender-related distress with later resolution/change; relevant psychiatric/neurodevelopmental comparators where scientifically justified.
