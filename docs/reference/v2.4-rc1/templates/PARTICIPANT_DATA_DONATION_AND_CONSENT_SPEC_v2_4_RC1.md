# OSLT Participant Data Donation and Consent Specification

> This is a research-design specification, not a participant-facing consent form and not legal advice. A sponsoring institution/REC and data-protection team must produce the final approved documents.

## Separate consent choices
Consent should be granular and revocable where applicable, with separate choices for: medical-record linkage; education linkage; platform exports; search/browser history; email/message content; interviews; future recontact; future related research; international/federated analysis.

## Minimum safeguards
- No collection from accounts/devices without the participant's explicit authorisation.
- Third-party communications minimised/redacted where feasible.
- Direct identifiers stored separately from research data.
- Pseudonymous research ID used in OSLT.
- Data minimisation by prespecified field/period.
- Clear withdrawal limits once anonymised aggregate outputs are released.
- Safeguarding/escalation protocol for disclosures of immediate risk.
- Special arrangements for children/young people including capacity/assent/parental authority as required by approved protocol.

## Donation manifest
For every donated archive record: participant pseudonym; source platform/system; export date; covered period; file list/hash; consent scope; third-party-content treatment; transformations; deletion/retention date.
