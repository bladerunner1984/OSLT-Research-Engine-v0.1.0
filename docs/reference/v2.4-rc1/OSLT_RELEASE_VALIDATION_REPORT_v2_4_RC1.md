## New fail-closed controls
1. Controlled/TRE data cannot be configured for raw export.
2. Consented participant data cannot be admitted without a consent reference.
3. Harvest plans require data minimisation, frozen field lists and retention rules.
4. Low information/sample regimes lower the attainable inference tier.
5. Field-level source availability is not assumed; all 640 mappings begin unverified until checked against source codebooks/APIs.
6. The proposition registry includes rival/null families and explicit falsifiers.

## Validation boundary

Those require approved empirical studies, preregistration/SAPs, real data, replication and independent review.
