  The tier was **not** lowered. At this tier resolution merged **5 clusters, all

## 3. Outcome dates and where they came from

`LegislationConnector.outcome_dates()` was run **live** against legislation.gov.uk
(queries: "Gender Recognition", "Equality Act", "Health and Care Act"). It succeeded; the
fallback list of repo-recorded enactment dates was not used. The 29 dates are enactment
years encoded in legislation.gov.uk identifiers, resolved to 1 January (the precision the
identifier carries), spanning 1990-01-01 to 2026-01-01. They are fixed by Parliament, not
chosen by the analyst.

## 6. Disposition

**The handoff's disposition STANDS.** MX09 (isolated, non-coupled processes) is favoured
over MD15 (structural coupling) on the 1,286-relation graph, against 10 independent
Parliament-fixed dates. Quadrupling the graph did not produce coupling; it produced more
disjoint single-tie-type components.

What would have had to be true for it to flip: at least one connected component of more
than two entities, spanning more than one non-UNKNOWN domain, joined by **more than one
relation type** — i.e. some entity appearing as both, say, a funding counterparty and a
contract or advisory counterparty under a shared strong identifier. Not one such entity
exists in this graph at this tier.

## 7. Bounds on the absence claim (this is not a claim about the world)

"No shared entity" is a statement about **this** graph, at **STRONG_IDENTIFIER**, over
**these five registers**. It is bounded by:

1. **Coverage.** Only 360Giving, UKRI Gateway to Research, Contracts Finder, GOV.UK
   publications, and UK Parliament written evidence are loaded. Company filings, board
   memberships, personnel overlap, grant sub-awards, correspondence, and non-UK registers
   are absent. Coupling running through any of those channels is invisible here.
2. **Identifier coverage.** 434 of 824 entities carry no strong identifier, so a genuine
   shared body appearing in two registers under two spellings **cannot** be joined at this
   tier. Absence of a bridge is partly a measurement limit, not only a property of the world.
   (Lowering the tier to close that gap is barred: every historical MD15 positive here
   evaporated once name-only merges were disallowed.)
3. **Domain coverage.** 647 of 824 entities are domain UNKNOWN and can never widen
   cross-system spread.
4. **Date precision.** Enactment dates resolve to 1 January of the enactment year; day-level
   precision would be invented.
5. **Register asymmetry.** ISSUES_GUIDANCE_TO (30) and CONTRACTS_WITH (103) are thin next to
   FUNDS (926), so the guidance and procurement arms have far less chance of intersecting
   anything.
