# OSLT executable roadmap

## Milestone 0 — repository foundation (this release)

Governed contracts, immutable lineage, CI, provenance, persistence, four public connectors, Pilot 1
skeleton, master synthesis contract and film evidence register.

## Milestone 1 — Pilot 1 denominator and coding system

- preregistered searches and inclusion/exclusion rules;
- registry/publication linkage;
- study-family and dataset-ancestry graph;
- neutral orientation taxonomy;
- blind human dual coding and adjudication;
- inter-rater reliability and coder-drift checks;
- correction/retraction updates.

## Milestone 2 — reproducible academic knowledge-production study

- frozen corpus and manifest;
- publication-selection, citation-network, funding and guideline-diffusion analyses;
- SUPPORT/CONTRADICT/RIVAL/NULL/BIAS_CRITIQUE/REPLICATION/CORRECTION_RETRACTION lanes;
- sensitivity analyses and independent methods review;
- documentary-ready released-claim dossier.

## Milestone 3 — public policy, guideline and historical kernels

- versioned professional guidance corpus;
- policy and parliamentary timeline;
- guideline-to-source citation ancestry;
- institutional relationship graph;
- cross-jurisdictional change-point candidates.

## Milestone 4 — restricted-data readiness

- executable TRE/SDE analysis packages;
- ethics, DPIA and data-access documents;
- disclosure-control result contracts;
- synthetic-data tests;
- no common-store raw person-level data.

## Milestone 5 — master synthesis qualification

- at least three independently validated kernel families;
- scope-alignment and dependency-collapse benchmarks;
- contradiction-resolution benchmark;
- model gateway qualification;
- independent scientific governance panel;
- release and documentary fact-check protocol.
